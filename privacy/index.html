Privacy, in plain English

Puzzl is a free puzzle site made by one person. You can play every game without an account. If you sign in, here's exactly what's kept and why.

If you just play

Your progress, streaks and stats are saved in your browser on your device. They don't leave your device.

If you sign in

  • Your email address, so we can send you a sign-in code. It's never shown to anyone, never shared and never used for marketing.
  • The name you pick, which appears on the public leaderboards.
  • Your daily scores (points and a short result like "Found in 2"), which make up the leaderboards.
  • Which private leagues you've joined, such as The Puzzl Crew.

Your name and scores are public on the leaderboards. Nothing else is.

Who looks after it

Accounts and scores are stored with Supabase. Sign-in emails are sent by Resend. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Puzzl has no adverts and no tracking.

Deleting your account

Tap your initial in the top corner of the homepage, then Delete my account. Your email, name and all your scores are removed straight away and can't be brought back.
